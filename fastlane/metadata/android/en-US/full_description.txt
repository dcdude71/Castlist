Castlist is a podcast player built on AntennaPod, the open-source podcast manager. It keeps everything AntennaPod does and adds a few features for people who listen a lot.

<b>Smart and manual playlists</b>
<ul>
<li>A smart playlist is a set of rules, one per podcast: how many episodes, the most recent or the oldest unplayed, and in which order. The playlist fills and updates itself.</li>
<li>Or fill a playlist by hand, like an extra queue: add episodes from any episode's menu, drag to reorder, play from any episode. The regular queue stays as it is.</li>
<li>Move on to the next playlist when one ends.</li>
<li>Keep a playlist's episodes downloaded automatically, or show only downloaded ones.</li>
<li>See the time left in each playlist, in the app, in the player and on the widget.</li>
<li>Start playlists from a home screen widget, a home screen icon or Android Auto.</li>
</ul>

<b>Episode filters</b>
<ul>
<li>Keep or skip episodes with conditions on the title, the description or the length: contains, doesn't contain, starts with, ends with, is exactly.</li>
<li>Any or all conditions, whole words, accents and case ignored, with a live preview of what is kept and skipped.</li>
<li>Filters decide what downloads automatically and what playlists use.</li>
</ul>

<b>Player</b>
<ul>
<li>A modern player with big controls and colours from the artwork, or the classic AntennaPod layout.</li>
<li>A fast-forward time per podcast, handy for skipping ads, used everywhere: player, lock screen, headphones, Android Auto, watch.</li>
<li>Layouts for foldables and large screens.</li>
</ul>

<b>Everything from AntennaPod</b>
Subscribe to any RSS feed, queue and inbox, automatic downloads and cleanup, chapters, sleep timer, playback speed, gpodder.net and Nextcloud sync, OPML import and export, and database backups. A backup from AntennaPod imports into Castlist as is.

Castlist collects no data. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the AntennaPod team. Please report problems on Castlist's GitHub page, not to AntennaPod.
