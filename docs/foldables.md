# Foldables and big screens

On a wide screen (an unfolded Pixel Fold or Galaxy Z Fold, a tablet, or a phone in landscape), Castlist uses the extra room. Everything rearranges itself as you fold and unfold, and you stay where you were.

## Player

With the Castlist player style, the show notes sit next to the artwork and controls instead of behind the Show notes button.

### Tabletop mode

Half-fold the phone and stand it on a table, held sideways: the artwork moves to the top half and big controls to the bottom half.

## Playlists

Your playlists on the left, the selected playlist's episodes on the right. Tap the pencil to edit: the rules move to the left, and a live preview on the right updates as you change them. Newly added episodes are briefly highlighted.

![Playlists on a big screen](images/foldable-playlists.png)

## Episode details beside the list

Tapping an episode anywhere (Inbox, Queue, Episodes, Downloads, Home, a podcast's page) opens its details on the right while the list stays on the left. Close them with ✕ or Back. Fold the phone and the episode goes full screen.

## Home screen widget

The [playlists widget](home-screen.md#playlists-widget) uses compact tiles on the narrow outer screen and wider ones when there is room.

## Subscriptions

When the phone is open (or on any screen at least 600 dp wide), the Subscriptions grid shows twice the number of columns you chose in its menu, so the covers stay about the same size. Folding the phone goes back to your number.
